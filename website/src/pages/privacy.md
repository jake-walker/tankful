## We don't collect your data

Tankful does not collect, receive, or share your personal information or fuel logs. The app has no cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking. This website also uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking.

## Sync is your choice

Data you enter stays on your device unless you setup sync with a Tracktor or LubeLogger server. If you do, Tankful sends data to the server you choose.

## App store analytics

Apple and Google may collect usage data and crash report through the App Store and Google Play, and make some of this information available to developers. This is collected by the respective company under its own privacy policy and your platform settings. Tankful adds no analytics of its own.
